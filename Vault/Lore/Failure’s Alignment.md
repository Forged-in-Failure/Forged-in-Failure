## Basic concept

The world of FiF revolves around the power of one’s soul and the will to achieve a goal. All magic comes from the soul.

Magic can be enhanced through armour, accessories, and skills. These don’t create power; they amplify it through stats and the confidence that strengthens willpower.

The player character is a hollow shell with their own soul, acting as a puppet for the player (us). Because two souls are present, the player character can respawn. Their combined will rivals an omniscient deity in raw force.

## Relevant info

- The island we play on is part of an archipelago. Until we defeat the boss assigned to the island (or until a later update), a fog surrounds it.
- The fog makes people forget their will: goals, dreams, hopes, and willpower fade until the desire to live leaves the body.
- Strong beings across the island pursue their goals by any means. The player can kill them in any order.
- If bosses grow stronger based on who you’ve already killed, that should be reflected in the lore.

## Worldbuilding ideas

- The unit of measurement is the length of a human head, because people believe the soul is stored in the brain.
- Undead creatures have damaged, unstable souls, giving them unpredictable properties (including imperfect respawning).
- Beings born or created without a soul can have one forcibly developed by the universe as a form of balance.
- The island of FiF is full of people who are failures to society, but they’re by no means weak.
- There are no traditional political borders across FiF. People control the area they live in, creating a semi-anarchist society with occasional authority figures in cities and villages.
- When magic was first discovered, humans tried to expand their lifespans. Those attempts split souls across time and space into tiny fragments that exist all at once.
- There are no documented cases of respawning working.
- Low-tier revivals have worked, but the revived person usually loses a piece of their soul to patch what was hurt, often causing lost limbs or abilities.
- The Skylite Isles used to be part of Nimbus Peaks, but long ago a powerful mage lifted them above the clouds to escape the Aether Spire.
- If someone attempts to transplant their soul into a new body, the universe usually contests them to prevent eternal life.
- The universe is a being beyond limits that keeps the world’s scales balanced. It is devoid of will and thought, with no malice or kindness.
- An Observer exists everywhere throughout time, ensuring nothing breaks the balance, and reports any “errors” to the universe.
