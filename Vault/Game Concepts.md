## Mechanics

### Classes

- **Archer (armor)** — Fast ranged DPS using bows/crossbows.
    - **Pros:** High move speed; high attack speed; weapon variety; ranged advantage.
    - **Cons:** Low HP/defense; weak team support; struggles up close; ammo-limited.
    - **Subclasses**
        - **Hunter:** Traps/CC + weak-point crit focus. (Mark, Trap, Insight)
        - **Arcane Archer:** Elemental/status shots; can spend mana for arrows when empty. (Rain, Homing, Piercing)
        - **Gunsmith:** Firearms burst damage (pistols/rifles/shotguns/cannons); heavy guns slow you; ammo-limited. (Quickdraw, Suppressing Fire, Deadeye)

## Enemies

- **Grinbloom**
    - Overview: A cheerful-looking bloom that fights with strangling vines and mind-warping spores.
    - Attacks:
        - **Vines / sharp tendrils:** Grabs the player to throw or suffocate them.
        - **Barbed tips:** The sharp ends can cut or stab for heavy damage.
    - Coated fungi petals:
        - Hallucinogenic effects if consumed (delirium, sweating, fatigue/weakness).
        - Gameplay: warped POV + reduced damage output.
    - Weak spot: Face (petals can fold up to hide it).

## Phenomena

- **Nimbus**
    - **Type:** Robloxian-shaped cloud entity
    - **Scale:** Massive compared to an average player
    - **Temperament:** Usually calm
    - **Perception:** Limited sight
    - **Environmental influence:** Weather and conditions shift with its mood
