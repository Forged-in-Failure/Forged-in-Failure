# Forged in Failure

An indie RPG set in the **Skylite Isles** — a fog-covered floating archipelago with no formal governance, populated by society's outcasts. Originally developed on Roblox/Luau, the project has fully migrated to **Godot (GDScript)** due to Roblox's poor moderation, platform neglect, and paywalling of previously free features.

![Watchtower concept render](./watchtower.png)

## About

The Skylite Isles are a scattered archipelago wrapped in a memory-eating fog. Until the boss tied to an island is defeated, that fog surrounds it — slowly stripping away the will, goals, and hope of anyone who lingers. All magic in this world draws from the soul, and the player character is a hollow shell housing two souls at once, which is why death isn't always the end.

Progression is open-order: strong beings across the isles can be fought in whatever sequence the player chooses, with some bosses growing stronger depending on who's already fallen.

## Core Systems

- **Soul / willpower-based magic** — armor, accessories, and skills amplify innate soul power rather than create it
- **Dual-soul protagonist** — enables respawn mechanics tied to death and revival, though full revival is rare and costly (lost limbs/abilities, unstable souls)
- **Fog mechanic** — erodes willpower over time and gates per-island boss fights
- **Open-order boss progression** — no fixed sequence; some bosses react to prior kills

## Classes

Four class trees, each with three subclasses:

- **Archer** — Fast ranged DPS (bows/crossbows). Subclasses: Hunter, Arcane Archer, Gunsmith
- **Mage**
- **Warrior**
- **Rogue**

## Notable Bosses & Creatures

- **Nimbus** — a massive, Robloxian-shaped cloud entity with a three-phase fight (wind → rain → lightning-enraged)
- **Grinbloom** — a cheerful-looking bloom that fights with strangling vines, barbed tendrils, and hallucinogenic spores; weak point is its face

## Development

This is a rotating-task team rather than siloed roles — contributors move across modeling, art, scripting, writing, and sound as needed. The GDScript migration is complete and the project is now in active implementation.

**Compensation:** Revenue share post-launch. No current funding.
